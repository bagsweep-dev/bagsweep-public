Three Steps to Autopilot Yield
Deploy a smart wallet, set your sweep policy, and let the protocol handle the rest.
01

Deploy Smart Wallet

Connect your wallet and deploy an ERC-4337 smart account via our factory. Your on-chain vault with deterministic CREATE2 addressing.

02

Set Sweep Policy

Choose what percentage to sweep, which tokens to monitor, and where profits go — USDG yield pool, tokenized stocks, or a 50/50 split.

03

Autonomous Execution

Our keeper monitors your positions, evaluates PnL in real-time, and submits gasless sweep transactions via bundled UserOps. Zero intervention needed.

Gasless Execution

All sweep transactions are sponsored by the BagSweep paymaster. You never pay gas for automated sweeps — the protocol covers it.

🛡

Non-Custodial

Your keys, your coins. The smart account is owned by your EOA. The keeper can only execute approved sweep operations — nothing more.

Real-Time PnL Tracking

DexScreener-powered price feeds evaluate your meme positions in real-time. Track unrealized gains, sweep history, and yield accrual from one dashboard.

Flexible Destinations
